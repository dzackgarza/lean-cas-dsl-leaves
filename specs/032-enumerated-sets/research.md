## Decisions

**R1. The adopted order is known mathematics.** No surveyed engine enumerates $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ in Mathlib's `Denumerable.ofNat` order. The order is known mathematics with a formal reference; its implementation is ordinary leaf work under LV6.
